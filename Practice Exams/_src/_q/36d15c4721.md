6\. (10 points) Consider an ordinary binary min-heap data structure $H$ with $n$ elements supporting the instructions INSERT and EXTRACT-MIN in $O(\log n)$ worst-case time. Give a potential function $\Phi(H)$ such that the amortized cost of INSERT is $O(\log n)$ and the amortized cost of EXTRACT-MIN is $O(1)$. The potential function should be zero when the heap is empty, and has positive value otherwise. Show that it works for both instructions.
