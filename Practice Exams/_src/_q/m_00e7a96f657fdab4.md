12\. **(5 points)** There are many types of elementary matrices. The properties are described in elementary matrices if

(1) a *nxn* matrix is obtained by interchanging two rows of $\mathbf{I}$.\
(2) a *nxn* matrix is obtained by multiplying a column of $\mathbf{I}$ by a nonzero constant.\
(3) a *nxn* matrix is obtained from $\mathbf{I}$ by adding a multiple of one row to another row.\
(4) a *nxn* permutation matrix is formed from the $\mathbf{I}$ by reordering its columns.\
(5) the determinate of *nxn* matrix is always 1.
