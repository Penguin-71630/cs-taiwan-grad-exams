14\. **(5 points)** Defining the $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ being $n$ column vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and let $\mathbf{A} = [\mathbf{a}_1\ \mathbf{a}_2\ \ldots\ \mathbf{a}_n]$,

(1) the vectors $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ will be linearly independent if and only if $\mathbf{A}$ is nonsingular.\
(2) the determinate of $\mathbf{A}$ is nonzero.\
(3) the vectors $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ will be linearly independent if and only if the null space $N(\mathbf{A}) = \{\mathbf{0}\}$.\
(4) the rank of A plus the nullity of A equals $n$.\
(5) the rank of $\mathrm{A}^{\mathrm{T}}$ equals the rank of A.
