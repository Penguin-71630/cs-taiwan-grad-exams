7\. (15 points) 3-SAT and SUBSET-SUM are two NP-complete problems. In 3-SAT, we are asked whether a given boolean formula $\phi$ is satisfiable, where $\phi$ is in 3-conjunctive normal form (3-CNF). In the SUBSET-SUM problem, we are given a finite set of integers, $S$, and a target integer $t$. We ask whether there is a subset $S'\subseteq S$ whose elements sum to $t$. It has been shown that 3-SAT is polynomial time reducible to SUBSET-SUM. Given the following boolean formula in 3-CNF,

$$\phi=(x_1\vee x_2\vee x_3)\wedge(x_1\vee\overline{x_2}\vee x_3)\wedge(\overline{x_1}\vee\overline{x_2}\vee\overline{x_3})$$

what is its corresponding SUBSET-SUM problem? (You need to show your construction process.)
