3\. (10%) Determine whether each of the following statements is true or false. Justify your answers.

(a) (2%) The propositions $(p \to q) \to r$ and $p \to (q \to r)$ are logically equivalent.\
(b) (2%) The proposition $(p \land (q \to \neg p)) \to \neg q$ is a tautology.\
(c) (2%) The argument form is valid:

$$\begin{array}{l} p \to r \\ p \lor q \\ \neg q \\ \therefore r \end{array}$$

(d) (2%) If $A=\{1,2,3\}$ and $B=\{1,3,4\}$, then $(1,4) \in \mathcal{P}(A \times B)$. ($\mathcal{P}(S)$ is the power set of a set $S$; $A \times B$ is the Cartesian product of the two sets $A$ and $B$.)\
(e) (2%) For all integers $a,b,c$ with $c \neq 0$, if $ac \mid bc$, then $a \mid b$.
