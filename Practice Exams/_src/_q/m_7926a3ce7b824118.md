14\. (6%) Given positive integers $C > 1$ and $n$, the $n$-tuple optimization problem is to determine whether there exist positive integers $c_1, c_2, \ldots, c_n$ such that $\prod_{i=1}^{n} c_i = C$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} c_i$ is minimized. The prime number problem is to determine whether a given positive integer is a prime number or not. Please prove that if the $n$-tuple optimization problem can be solved in polynomial time, then the prime number problem can be solved in polynomial time.
