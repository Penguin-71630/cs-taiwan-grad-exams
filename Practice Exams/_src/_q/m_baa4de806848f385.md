13\. This question is bout Floyd-Warshall algorithm .

(36). The following sequence of statements is to explain how the recursive formulation of Floyd-Warshall algorithm for all-pairs shortest-paths problem is conceived and derived in its first step. Unfortunately, there is a minor error in the writing of one statement. You will identify the error and select the statement that contains that error.

(A) Assume that the vertices of G are $V=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$. Consider a subset $\{1,2,\ldots,k\}$ of vertices for some k. An intermediate vertex of a simple path $p=\langle v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_m \rangle$ is any vertex of p other than $v_1$ or $v_m$.\
(B) Let d(i,j,k) be the weight of a shortest path from vertex i to vertex j for which all vertices are in the set $\{1,2,\ldots,k\}$.\
(C) Then we have $d(i,j,0) = w_{ij}$, where $w_{ij}$ denotes the weight of the edge from vertex i to vertex j.\
(D) And we have d(i,j,k) = min( d(i,j,k-1), d(i,k,k-1) + d(k,j,k-1) ), where $1 \leq k$.\
(E) Finally, the weight of the shortest path from vertex i to vertex j in the given graph G is simply d(i,j,n), which can be computed via the recursive formulation above.
