4 Consider the matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$.

(a) (2 points) Compute the dimensions of the column space $\mathrm{C}(\mathbf{A})$ and the null-space $\mathrm{N}(\mathbf{A})$.\
(b) (2 points) Give an orthogonal basis for $\mathrm{C}(\mathbf{A})$ and $\mathrm{N}(\mathbf{A})$, respectively.\
(c) (2 points) Find a projection matrix that projects any vector $b \in R^3$ onto the column space $\mathrm{C}(\mathbf{A})$.\
(d) (4 points) Find the least-squares solutions to the problem $\mathbf{Ax} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.\
(e) (5 points) Find the minimum-length, least-squares solution to the problem $\mathrm{A}\mathrm{x} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$.
