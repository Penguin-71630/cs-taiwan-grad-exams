18\. (5 points) The transitive closure $R^*$ of a relation $R$ can be calculated by Warshall's algorithm. The pseudocode of Warshall's algorithm is below.

```
1    Procedure Warshall (M_R: n × n zero-one matrix of R)
2    W := M_R
3    for k := 1 to n
4    begin
5        for i := 1 to n
6        begin
7            for j := 1 to n
8            w_ij := w_ij ∨ (w_ik ∧ w_kj)
9        end
10   end {W = [w_ij] is M_R*, the 0-1 matrix of R*}
```
