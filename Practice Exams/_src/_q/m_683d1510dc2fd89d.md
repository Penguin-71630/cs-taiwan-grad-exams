4\. (10%) Let vectors $\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n$ be represented as $\boldsymbol{x} = (x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)^T$. Which of the following sets is a subspace of $\boldsymbol{R}^n$?

(a) $\{\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n \mid \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 = n^2\}$\
(b) $\{\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n \mid x_1 = 1\}$\
(c) $\{\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n \mid \sum_{i=1}^{n} \alpha_i x_i = min_i(\alpha_i)\}$ for some known constants $\alpha_i \in \boldsymbol{R}$.\
(d) $\{\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n \mid x_{2i} = x_{2i-1}, \forall i = 1, \ldots, n/2\}$, where $n$ is assumed to be even in this case.\
(e) $\{\boldsymbol{x} \in \boldsymbol{R}^n \mid x_{2i} - x_{2i-1} = 1, \forall i = 1, \ldots, n/2\}$, where $n$ is assumed to be even in this case.
