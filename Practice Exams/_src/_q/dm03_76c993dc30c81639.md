For problems 16-18, the credit points of each problem are noted in the question sets, and the total points are 25 points. **Calculation processes do not get points.**

16\. Little Red needs to solve a linear nonhomogeneous recurrence relation $a_n = 4a_{n-1} - 3a_{n-2} + 4$. Please answer the following questions for him/her.

(A) (2 points) What is the constant $c$ such that $a_n = cn$ is a solution of $a_n = 4a_{n-1} - 3a_{n-2} + 4$?\
(B) (3 points) What is the characteristic equation and characteristic roots of the homogeneous recurrence relation $a_n = 4a_{n-1} - 3a_{n-2}$?\
(C) (5 points) Given $a_1 = -9$ and $a_2 = -5$, find the solution of $a_n = 4a_{n-1} - 3a_{n-2} + 4$.
