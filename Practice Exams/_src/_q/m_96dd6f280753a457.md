8\. This question is about Sorting:

(22) Which of the following 3 statement(s) is(are) true?\
(a) The worst case of quick sort takes $O(n^2)$ time.\
(b) The worst case of the randomized quick sort takes $O(n^2)$ time.\
(c) Quick sort is divide and conquer approach. After partitioning, we don't spend time for the merging step.\
(A), (a) and (b) are true, but (c) is not.\
(B) (a) and (c) are true, but (b) is not.\
(C) (b) and (c) are true, but (a) is not.\
(D) all of (a), (b), and (c) are true.\
(E) Only (a) is true.
