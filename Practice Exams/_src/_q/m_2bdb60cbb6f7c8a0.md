7\. (10%) Let $V$ be a vector space over a scalar field $F$. For any subset $S$ of $V$, let span($S$) consist of the vectors of $V$ that can be written as $a_1x_1 + a_2x_2 + \cdots + a_nx_n$ with $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n \in F$ and $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in S$ for some positive integer $n$. Prove that span($S$) is the unique subspace of $V$ such that any subspace of $V$ containing $S$ has to contain span($S$). Specifically, you have to show that (a) span($S$) is a subspace of $V$, (b) if $U$ is a subspace of $V$ with $S \subseteq U$, then span($S$) $\subseteq U$, and (c) there is no other subspace of $V$ satisfying property (b).
