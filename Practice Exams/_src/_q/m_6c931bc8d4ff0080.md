12\. **(5 points)** Let $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$, $\mathbf{C}$, and $\mathbf{D}$ be $n \times n$ matrices with $\mathbf{A}$ invertible. Prove that $\det\begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{A} & \mathbf{B} \\ \mathbf{C} & \mathbf{D} \end{bmatrix} = (\det\mathbf{A})\det(\mathbf{D} - \mathbf{C}\mathbf{A}^{-1}\mathbf{B})$.
