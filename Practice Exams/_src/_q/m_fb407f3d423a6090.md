24\. Let G= (V, E) be an undirected graph with $n$ vertices, where $n>1$, and M be the adjacency matrix of G. Which of the following statements is not correct?

(A) M is a 2-dimension $n \times n$ array.\
(B) M is symmetric.\
(C) The degree of any vertex $i$ is equal to the $i^{\text{th}}$ row sum.\
(D) The graph has an even number of vertices of odd degree.\
(E) The number of edges of G is equal to $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n} \mathrm{M}(i,j)$.
