5\. (5%) Consider a demand-paging memory management system that uses the additional-reference-bits algorithm for page replacement. Let $b$ be the 8-bit byte that we keep for a page $P$. Let $(t_1, t_2, t_3, \ldots)$ be the sequence of times at which $b$ is updated. Suppose that the *decimal* value of $b$ after the update at time $t_{20}$ is 147. Assume that the numbers of references to the page $P$ at the time intervals $(t_{20}, t_{21})$, $(t_{21}, t_{22})$, $(t_{22}, t_{23})$, $(t_{23}, t_{24})$, $(t_{24}, t_{25})$, $(t_{25}, t_{26})$, $(t_{26}, t_{27})$, $(t_{27}, t_{28})$ are, respectively, 0, 1, 2, 0, 3, 0, 4, 2. What is the *decimal* value of $b$ after the update at $t_{23}$? Detailed explanation is necessary.
