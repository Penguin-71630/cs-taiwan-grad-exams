4\. (10%) Define function $f : \mathbb{R}^2 \times \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ as

$$f(x, y) = x_1 y_1 - x_1 y_2 - x_2 y_1 + 4 x_2 y_2,$$

for any vector $x$ (respectively, $y$) with standard coordinate $(x_1, x_2)$ (respectively, $(y_1, y_2)$). Let

$$u = (1, 0).$$

Find a vector $v$ such that

$$f(x, y) = s_1 t_1 + s_2 t_2,$$

where $(s_1, s_2)$ (respectively, $(t_1, t_2)$) is the coordinate of $x$ (respectively, $y$) with respect to the ordered basis of $\mathbb{R}^2$ consisting of $u$ and $v$.
