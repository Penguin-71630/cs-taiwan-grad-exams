11\. (10%) Let $V$ be the vector space consisting of all $2 \times 2$ real matrices. Let $\beta$ be the standard ordered basis consisting of $\beta_1, \ldots, \beta_4$ in that order, where

$$\beta_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},\ \beta_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix},\ \beta_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix},\ \beta_4 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}.$$

The function $f$ defined by

$$f(X, Y) = \mathrm{tr}(X) \cdot \mathrm{tr}(Y)$$

for any matrices $X$ and $Y$ in $V$ is known to be in bilinear form. What is the matrix representation of the function $f$ in bilinear form with respect to the ordered basis $\beta$? (The answer is a $4 \times 4$ real matrix. That is why you need a larger cell for this problem in the table for your answers on the first page of your answer sheet.)
