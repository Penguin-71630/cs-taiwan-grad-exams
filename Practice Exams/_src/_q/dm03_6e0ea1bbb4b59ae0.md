8\. (5 points) Given a matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 & 1 & 5 \\ 2 & 3 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, which of the following statements are TRUE?

(A) $\mathbf{A}$ has a column space of dimension 3.\
(B) The column space of $\mathbf{A}$ is identical to the column space of matrix $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 5 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$.\
(C) The left nullspace of $\mathbf{A}$ is a line in $\mathrm{R}^3$.\
(D) $\mathbf{A}^{\mathrm{T}}\mathbf{A}$ is invertible.\
(E) If the two systems $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{c}$ have the same shortest (minimal 2-norm) least-squares solution, then the projections of $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$ onto the column space of $\mathbf{A}$ must be identical.
