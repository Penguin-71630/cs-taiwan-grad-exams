4\. (5%)

Which of the followings are true?

(a) The formula $\det\begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix} = \det(A)\det(D) - \det(C)\det(B)$ always hold for square matrices $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$.\
(b) The formula $\det(A^k) = (\det(A))^k$ always hold for positive integer $k$ and square matrix $A$.\
(c) The formula $\det(A) = \det(-A)$ always hold for square matrix $A$.\
(d) The formula $\det(A^TA) > 0$ always hold for nonsingular matrix $A$.\
(e) The formula $\det(A) = 1$ always hold for orthogonal matrix $A$.
