17\. Let d and k be two non-negative integers, where k > d >= 0. A dk-binary sequence is a binary sequence that satisfies the following two constraints:

(a) d constraint: two 1’s are separated by a run of consecutive 0 of length at least d.\
(b) k constraint: any run of consecutive 0’s is of length at most k.

For example, 010010001001 is a dk-binary sequence of length 12 with d=0 and k=3. Let n indicate the length of dk-binary sequence.

(47). Given n=6, d=0, k=6, then how many dk-binary sequences are there?

(A) 8\
(B) 16\
(C) 32\
(D) 64\
(E) 128.
