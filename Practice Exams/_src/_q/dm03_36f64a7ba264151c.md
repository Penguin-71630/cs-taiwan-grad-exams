5\. (5 points) The $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ be $n$ column vectors in $R^n$ and let $\mathbf{A} = [\mathbf{a}_1\ \mathbf{a}_2\ \ldots\ \mathbf{a}_n]$.

(A) The vectors $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ will be linearly independent if and only if **A** is singular.\
(B) The vectors $\mathbf{a}_1, \mathbf{a}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{a}_n$ will be linearly independent if and only if $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{0}$ has only the trivial solution $\mathbf{x} = [0\ 0\ \ldots\ 0]^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(C) The rank of **A** plus the nullity of **A** equals $n$.\
(D) The rank of **A** equals the rank of $\mathbf{A}^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(E) All of the above are true.
