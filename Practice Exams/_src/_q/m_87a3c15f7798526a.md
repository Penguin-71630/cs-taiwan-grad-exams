Let $\mathbf{A}_n = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 1 & 3 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 3 \end{bmatrix}_{n \times n}$ be the triple diagonal matrix

(a) (4 points) Express the determinant $\det(\mathbf{A}_n)$ in terms of $\det(\mathbf{A}_{n-1})$ and $\det(\mathbf{A}_{n-2})$\
(b) (6 points) Evaluate $\det(\mathbf{A}_n)$ as a function of $n$.
