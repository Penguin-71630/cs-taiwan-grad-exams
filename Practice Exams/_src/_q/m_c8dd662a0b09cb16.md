0x1FD0, 0x2CCC, 0x12A6, 0x332B, 0x05E3, 0x2640, 0x935B, 0x332D, 0x05F0, 0x2733, 0x2633, 0xA283, 0x3525, 0x27AC, 0x2653, 0xA36F, 0xAF8C, 0x5D80, 0x3C25, 0x2853

Which of the following are correct?

(a) With four frames, the page faults number for the FIFO replacement algorithms is 9.\
(b) With four frames, the page faults number for the least-recently-used (LRU) replacement algorithms is 7.\
(c) With three frames, the page faults number for the optimal replacement algorithms is 7.\
(d) Assume that the process is allocated at the No.0 to 3 frames (four frames in total); the physical memory address of the last access "0x2853" is "0x1853" by the LRU algorithm.\
(e) Assume that the process is allocated at the No.0 to 2 frames (three frames in total); the physical memory address of the access "0x2733" is "0x1F33" by the optimal algorithm.
