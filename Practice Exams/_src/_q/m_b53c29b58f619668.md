39\. A hiker faces the Knapsack problem. There are 7 items to be packed into the knapsack, each with value $v_i$ and weight $w_i$ as shown in the following table.

| $i$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| $v_i$ | 8 | 6 | 15 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 9 |
| $w_i$ | 2 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 6 |

The knapsack, which is initially empty, can hold a maximum weight of 16, so some item(s) must be left behind. The optimality criterion is to maximize the total value of the items that are placed in the knapsack. The hiker fills the knapsack one item at a time. Now consider the following two cases: (a) fractions of items cannot be packed, and (2) fractions of items can be packed. What are the optimal values of the items that are packed in these two cases respectively?

Give the answer in the form of (no-fractions-allowed, fractions-allowed).

(A) (28, 30.5)\
(B) (37, 40)\
(C) (30, 34)\
(D) (38, 40)\
(E) (34, 38.5)
