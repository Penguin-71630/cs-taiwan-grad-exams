13\. **(5 points)** If there are two *nxn* matrices $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$ and one scalar $r$,

(1) $\det(\mathbf{A}) = \det(\mathbf{B})$ implies $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{B}$.\
(2) $\det(r\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B}) = r\det(\mathbf{A})+\det(\mathbf{B})$.\
(3) $\det((\mathbf{AB})^{\mathrm{T}}) = \det(\mathbf{A})\det(\mathbf{B})$.\
(4) $\det(\mathbf{A}) = \det(\mathbf{B})$ where $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are row equivalent matrices.\
(5) $\det((\mathbf{AB})^{-1}) = \det(\mathbf{A}^{-1})\det(\mathbf{B}^{-1})$.
