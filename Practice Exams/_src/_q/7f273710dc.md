9\. (5%)

Suppose $A_{3\times 3}$ has three distinct eigenvalues 0, 1, 3 with corresponding eigenvectors $\mathbf{u}$, $\mathbf{v}$, $\mathbf{w}$. Which of the following statements are true?

(a) The rank of $A$ must be 2.\
(b) The matrix $A^2 - I$ is invertible.\
(c) $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ must span the column space of $A$.\
(d) The least-squares error of $A\mathbf{x} = 2\mathbf{v} + 3\mathbf{w} + \mathbf{u}$ must be $\|\mathbf{u}\|^2$.
