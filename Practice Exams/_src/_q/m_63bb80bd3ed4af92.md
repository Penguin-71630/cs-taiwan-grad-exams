11\. **(5 points)** A *nxn* matrix $\mathbf{A}$ is said to be nonsingular, if

(1) $\mathbf{A}$ is non-invertible.\
(2) there exists a multiplicative inverse matrix $\mathbf{B}$ such that $\mathbf{A}$ is row equivalent to $\mathbf{B}$.\
(3) the determinate of $\mathbf{A}$ is nonzero.\
(4) $\mathbf{Ax}=\mathbf{0}$ where $\mathbf{x}$ is nonzero vector.\
(5) $\mathbf{A}$ is row equivalent to $\mathbf{I}$.
