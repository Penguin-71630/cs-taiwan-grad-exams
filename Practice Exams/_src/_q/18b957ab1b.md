Consider a matrix $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 & 5 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$ and a system of linear equations $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b} = [-1, 3, 1]^T$. Which of the following vectors $\mathbf{c}$ will make the two systems $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}$ and $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ have the same least-square error.

(a) $\mathbf{c} = [0, -1, -1]^T$.\
(b) $\mathbf{c} = [3, 2, 2]^T$.\
(c) $\mathbf{c} = [1, 0, -1]^T$.\
(d) $\mathbf{c} = [2, 1, 2]^T$.\
(e) $\mathbf{c} = [1, 2, 1]^T$.
