6\. (**5 points**) Assume $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$, and $\mathbf{C}$ are respectively $m \times p$, $p \times q$, and $q \times n$ matrices, and $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{ABC}$. Let $\mathbf{M}^{kl} = \boldsymbol{a}_k \boldsymbol{c}^l$ for $1 \leq k \leq p$ and $1 \leq l \leq q$, here $\boldsymbol{a}_k$ and $\boldsymbol{c}^l$ respectively denote the $k$-th column vector of $\mathbf{A}$ and the $l$-th row vector of $\mathbf{C}$. Prove that $\mathbf{M} = \sum_{k=1}^{p} \sum_{l=1}^{q} b_{kl} \mathbf{M}^{kl}$.
