2\. Assume we have three significant decimal digits, and the accuracy of a floating number is to the second decimal place. Round the value of $2.85 \times 10^4 + 5.16 \times 10^6$ to the nearest decimal number with three significant decimal digits, first with guard and round digits (V_GR), and then without them (V_NoGR).

(a) The value of V_GR is $5.19 \times 10^6$.\
(b) The value of V_NoGR is $5.17 \times 10^6$.\
(c) The ulp of V_GR is 0.15.\
(d) Sticky bit is another scheme to improve accuracy by setting it to the value of performing Boolean XOR operation on all bits to the right of the round bit.
