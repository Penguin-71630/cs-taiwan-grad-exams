18\. In the RSA asymmetric cryptosystem,

$n = pq$;\
$ed \bmod (p-1)(q-1) = 1$, where $e$ is the public key, and $d$ is the private key.\
Encrypting plaintext: $C = M^e \bmod n$;\
Decrypting ciphertext: $M = C^e \bmod n$;\
Choose the correct statements:

(a) $n$ is public information\
(b) $p$ is public information\
(c) $q$ must be a prime number\
(d) $M$ must be relatively prime to $n$\
(e) $(p-1)(q-1)$ is public information

> 註：解密式依原卷照錄（原卷印為 $C^e$）。
