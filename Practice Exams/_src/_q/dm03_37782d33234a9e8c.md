3\. (5 points) There are many types of elementary matrices. Which of the following definitions are elementary matrices? (**I**: identity matrix)

(A) An $nxn$ matrix is obtained by interchanging two rows of **I**.\
(B) An $nxn$ matrix is obtained by multiplying a row of **I** by a nonzero constant.\
(C) An $nxn$ matrix is obtained from **I** by adding a multiple of one row to another row.\
(D) An $nxn$ matrix is formed from **I** by reordering its columns.\
(E) All of the above are elementary matrices.
