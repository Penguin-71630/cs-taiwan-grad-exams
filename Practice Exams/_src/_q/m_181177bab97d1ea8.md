14\. [10%] Let G = (V, E) be a connected, undirected graph with edge-weight function w:E$\rightarrow$R, and assume that all the edge weights are distinct. Consider a cycle ($v_1$, $v_2$,...,$v_k$, $v_{k+1}$) in G, where $v_{k+1}$=$v_1$, and let ($v_i$,$v_{i+1}$) be the edge in the cycle with the largest edge weight. Prove that ($v_i$, $v_{i+1}$) does not belong to the minimum spanning tree T of G.
