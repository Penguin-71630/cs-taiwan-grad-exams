2\. (15 points) Assume that $f(n) = O(g(n))$ with $g(n) \geq 2$ for all $n$. Are the following claims true or false?

(1) (3 points) $f(n) + g(n) = O(g(n))$.\
(2) (3 points) $f(n) \cdot g(n) = O(g(n) \log_2 g(n))$.\
(3) (3 points) $\sqrt{f(n)} = O(\sqrt{g(n)})$.\
(4) (3 points) $\log_2 f(n) = O(\log_2 g(n))$.\
(5) (3 points) $2^{f(n)} = O(2^{g(n)})$.
