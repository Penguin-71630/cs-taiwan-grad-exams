3\. (17%) **Answer each of the following questions.**

(A) (5%) Let x and y be integers and m be a positive integer, then x is congruent to y modulo m if ( x - y ) is divisible by m, we denote this fact as $x \text{≡} y \ \mathbf{mod}\ m$. Fermat's Little Theorem states that if p is prime and z is an integer not divisible by p then $z^{p-1} \text{≡} 1 \ \mathbf{mod}\ p$.

Use Fermat's Little Theorem to compute\
$3^{302}\ \mathbf{mod}\ 5$,\
$3^{302}\ \mathbf{mod}\ 7$,\
$3^{302}\ \mathbf{mod}\ 11$,\
respectively.

(B) (5%) Use the results in (A) and Chinese Remainder Theorem to find $3^{302}\ \mathbf{mod}\ 385$. (Note that $385 = 5 \bullet 7 \bullet 11$)

(C) (7%) Solve the simultaneous recurrent relations

$$\begin{aligned} a_n &= 3a_{n-1} + 2b_{n-1} \\ b_n &= a_{n-1} + 2b_{n-1} \end{aligned}$$

with $a_0=2$, $b_0=1$
