14\. (5 points) In the following statements, which are TRUE?

(A) If 1+1 = 2 or 1+1 = 3, then 2+2 = 3 and 2+2 = 4.\
(B) $p \to (q \to r)$ and $p \to (q \wedge r)$ are equivalent.\

(D) The proposition $((p \to q) \wedge \neg p) \to \neg q$ is a tautology.\
(E) The proposition $((p \to \neg q) \wedge q) \to \neg p$ is a tautology.
