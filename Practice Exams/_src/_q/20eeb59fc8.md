2\. (5%)

Which of the followings are true?

(a) There exists a matrix $A$ such that $A\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \\ 5 \end{bmatrix}$.\
(b) A system of three linear equations in two unknowns is always inconsistent.\
(c) If $A$ and $B$ are nonsingular $n \times n$ matrices, then $AB$ is also nonsingular.\
(d) If $A$ and $B$ are nonsingular $n \times n$ matrices, then $A + B$ is also nonsingular.\
(e) If $A$, $B$, and $A + B$ are nonsingular $n \times n$ matrices, then $A^{-1} + B^{-1}$ is also nonsingular.
