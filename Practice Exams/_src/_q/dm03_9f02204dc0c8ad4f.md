9\. (5 points) Consider a matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 & 1 & 5 \\ 2 & 3 & 0 & 4 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ and a system of linear equations $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{b} = (0,3,3)^{\mathrm{T}}$. Which of the following vectors $\mathbf{c}$ will make $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{c}$ have a least-squares error (i.e., $\min_{\mathbf{x} \in R^4} \|\mathbf{Ax} - \mathbf{c}\|_2^2$) smaller than that of $\mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{b}$ (i.e., $\min_{\mathbf{x} \in R^4} \|\mathbf{Ax} - \mathbf{b}\|_2^2$)?

(A) $\mathbf{c} = (3,3,3)^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(B) $\mathbf{c} = (2,1,1)^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(C) $\mathbf{c} = (6,1,1)^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(D) $\mathbf{c} = (10,1,2)^{\mathrm{T}}$.\
(E) $\mathbf{c} = (1,4,7)^{\mathrm{T}}$.
