2\. (18 points) Give tight asymptotic bounds for the following recurrences. You need not justify your answers.

(a) (6 points) $T(n) = \begin{cases} 3T(n/8) + \sqrt{n} & \text{if } n > 1 \\ 1 & \text{if } n = 1, \end{cases}$

(b) (6 points) $T(n) = \begin{cases} T(\sqrt{n}) + 1 & \text{if } n > 2 \\ 1 & \text{if } n \le 2, \end{cases}$

(c) (6 points) $T(n) = \begin{cases} 8T(n/2) + \Theta(1) & \text{if } n^2 > M \\ M & \text{if } n^2 \le M, \end{cases}$ where $M$ is a variable independent from $n$.
