4\. (5 points) If there are two $nxn$ matrices **A**, **B** and one scalar $r$,

(A) $\det(\mathbf{A}) = \det(\mathbf{B})$ implies $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{B}$.\
(B) $\det(r\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B}) = r\det(\mathbf{A})+\det(\mathbf{B})$.\
(C) $\det((\mathbf{AB})^{\mathrm{T}}) = \det(\mathbf{A})\det(\mathbf{B})$.\
(D) $\det(\mathbf{A}) = \det(\mathbf{B})$ where **A** and **B** are row equivalent matrices.\
(E) none of the above is true.
