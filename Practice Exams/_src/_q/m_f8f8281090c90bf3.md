Suppose $\mathbf{A}$ is the sum of three matrices: $\mathbf{A} = \boldsymbol{u}\boldsymbol{v}^{\mathrm{T}} + \boldsymbol{v}\boldsymbol{w}^{\mathrm{T}} + \boldsymbol{w}\boldsymbol{u}^{\mathrm{T}}$. If $x_1\boldsymbol{u} + x_2\boldsymbol{v} + x_3\boldsymbol{w} = \mathbf{0}$ has a nonzero solution $(x_1, x_2, x_3)$, what is the maximum possible $\mathrm{rank}(\mathbf{A})$? and why?
