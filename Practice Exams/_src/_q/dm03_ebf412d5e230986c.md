6\. (5%)

Let $A$, $B$ and $C$ be three matrices, and $AB = C$. Which of the followings are true?

(a) The row space of $C$ is a subspace of the row space of $A$.\
(b) The column space of $C$ is a subspace of the column space of $A$.\
(c) The row space of $C$ is a subspace of the row space of $B$.\
(d) If the column vectors of $A$ are linearly dependent, then the column vectors of $C$ are linearly dependent.\
(e) If the row vectors of $A$ are linearly dependent, then the row vectors of $C$ are linearly dependent.
