8\. Let matrices **A**, **B**, and **C** be square matrices. Choose the incorrect arguments

(A) The determinant $\det(\mathbf{AB}) = \det(\mathbf{A})\det(\mathbf{B})$\
(B) Let matrix **A** can be decomposed into $\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{QR}$, where **Q** is the orthogonal matrix and **R** is the upper triangular matrix. The determinant $\det(\mathbf{A}) = \det(\mathbf{R})$.\
(C) Let matrix **A** be diagonalizable; that is, $\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{X}^{-1}\mathbf{DX}$. The determinant $\det(\mathbf{A})=\det(\mathbf{D})$.\
(D) Let matrices **A** and **B** are similar. The determinant $\det(\mathbf{A})=\det(\mathbf{B})$.\
(E) Let matrix **B** be the Hermitian transpose of matrix **A**; that is $\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{A}^{\mathbf{H}}$. The determinant $\det(\mathbf{A})=\det(\mathbf{B})$.
