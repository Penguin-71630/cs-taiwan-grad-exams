4\. (10 points) You are given a **convex polygon** $P=\{v_1,v_2,\ldots,v_n\}$, where $v_i, 1\le i\le n$, are the vertices that form the polygon. In a convex polygon, each internal angle is less than or equal to 180 degrees and each line segment between two vertices remains inside or on the boundary of the polygon.

A triangulation of a convex polygon is a set of non-intersecting diagonals that partitions the polygon into triangles. We define that the weight of a triangulation is the sum of the lengths of the perimeters of all component triangles. The minimum triangulation problem is to find the triangulation of the given polygon that has the minimum weight.
