5\. In a vector space $V$, we have the two sets $S = \{\mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{u}_n\}$ and $Q = \{\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{v}_m\}$. Which of these statements can be true?

(A) $S$ is linearly independent, $Q$ spans $V$, and $n > m$.\
(B) $V$ has dimension $n$, $Q$ spans $V$, and $m \geq n$.\
(C) $S$ and $Q$ are bases for $V$, and $n = m$.\
(D) $S$ is linearly independent, $Q$ is a subset of $S$ *and* $Q$ is linearly dependent.\
(E) $S$ is linearly dependent, $Q$ is a subset of $S$ *and* $Q$ is linearly independent.
