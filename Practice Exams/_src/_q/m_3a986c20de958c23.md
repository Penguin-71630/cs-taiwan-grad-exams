13\. Solve the linear recurrence relation $a_n = 2a_{n-1} - a_{n-2} + 6n - 8$ with $a_0 = 5$ and $a_1 = 10$ by the following steps.

a\. **(2 points)** Find the characteristic equation of the associated homogeneous recurrence relation $a_n = 2a_{n-1} - a_{n-2}$.\
b\. **(3 points)** Find the solutions $a_n^{(h)}$ of the associated homogeneous recurrence relation.\
c\. **(5 points)** Find a particular solution $a_n^{(p)}$ of the nonhomogeneous recurrence relation $a_n = 2a_{n-1} - a_{n-2} + 6n - 8$.\
d\. **(2 points)** Use the initial condition $a_0 = 5$ and $a_1 = 10$ to find the solution.
