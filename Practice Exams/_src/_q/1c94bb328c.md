Which of the followings are true?

(a) Let $L : \mathbf{R}^n \to \mathbf{R}^m$ be a linear transformation. If $A$ is the standard matrix representation of $L$, then an $n \times n$ matrix $B$ will also be a matrix representation of $L$ if and only if $B$ is similar to $A$.\
(b) Let $L : V \to W$ be a linear transformation. $\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2, \ldots, \mathbf{v}_k$ are linearly dependent in $V$, if and only if $L(\mathbf{v}_1), L(\mathbf{v}_2), \ldots, L(\mathbf{v}_k)$ are linearly dependent in $W$.\
(c) The transition matrix from one basis to another must be nonsingular, and a matrix representation of a linear transformation can be singular.\
(d) Any two matrices have the same trace if and only if they are similar.\
(e) Let $[\mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_2]$ and $[\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2]$ be ordered bases for $\mathbf{R}^2$, where $\mathbf{u}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}$, $\mathbf{u}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 7 \end{bmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{v}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$, $\mathbf{v}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix}$.
