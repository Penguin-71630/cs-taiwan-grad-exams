5\. (5 points) The Fourier Transform provides the means of transforming a signal defined in the time domain into one defined in the frequency domain. The Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) can be viewed as a special case of the Fourier Transform that requires the signal to be discrete and of finite duration. DFT can be implemented with the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithm, a special algorithm with considerable savings in computational time. Given the efficiency of FFT, it is also commonly used to approximate the Fourier Transform of a continuous signal. There are some limitations inherent in this approach, though. Which of the following phenomena is not the result in errors between the FFT-computed and the desired transform: (a) aliasing, (b) leakage, (c) the picket-fence effect, (d) linkage.
