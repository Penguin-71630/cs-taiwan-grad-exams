8\. (6 points) Given the recurrence relations

$$\begin{aligned} a_n &= 2b_{n-1} + 1 \\ b_n &= a_{n-1} + 2b_{n-1} + 2 \qquad n \geq 1 \end{aligned}$$

with the boundary conditions $a_0 = 0$ and $b_0 = 0$.

What is the solution of $a_n$?

Suffice it to write down the solution; no computations are required.
