3\. Given the IEEE-754 single precision floating-point operand format as follows,

| Sign (s) | Exponent (e) | Fraction (f) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 bit | 8 bits | 23 its |

(a) The decimal number $-13.5$ is represented as 1 10000011 10110000000000000000000\
(b) The largest positive normalized number is $+(1-2^{-23}) \times 2^{+127}$.\
(c) The smallest positive denormalized number is $+2^{-149}$.\
(d) Exponents with all 1's are reserved for $\pm\infty$ and NaN (Not a Number).

> 註：表格中「23 its」為原卷所印。
