(a) Minimax Dijkstra from $v_a$, relaxing $d(v)\gets\min\{d(v),\max(d(u),w_{uv})\}$; return the predecessor path to $v_b$. Time $O((|V|+|E|)\log|V|)$ with a binary heap.\
(b)(1) Treat edges as undirected; BFS finds a shortest $v_a$–$v_b$ path. Orient its edges toward $v_b$ and all other edges arbitrarily; $O(|V|+|E|)$.\
(b)(2) Treat edges as undirected; Dijkstra finds a minimum-weight $v_a$–$v_b$ path. Orient its edges toward $v_b$ and all other edges arbitrarily; $O((|V|+|E|)\log|V|)$ with a binary heap.
