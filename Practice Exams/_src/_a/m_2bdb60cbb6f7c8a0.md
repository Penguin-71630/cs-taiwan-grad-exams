(a) Closure under finite linear combinations; use the usual convention $\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$.\
(b) Every subspace containing $S$ is closed under its finite linear combinations.\
(c) The unique smallest subspace containing $S$ is $\displaystyle\bigcap_{U\le V,\,S\subseteq U}U=\operatorname{span}(S)$. [?] Literal property (b) alone does not ensure uniqueness; the candidate must also contain $S$.
> 註：The Drive solution assumes that the candidate contains $S$; without this extra condition, (c) is false since $\{0\}$ also satisfies the containment property. The stated definition also needs the empty-sum convention when $S=\varnothing$.
