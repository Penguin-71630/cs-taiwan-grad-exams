(a) One table for the whole system with one entry per physical frame storing <pid, page#>; the logical address <pid, p, d> is searched (associatively/hashed) in the table, and the index i of the matching entry gives the physical address <i, d>.\
(b) Page number p is first looked up in the TLB; on a hit the frame f comes from the TLB, on a miss from the page table (via PTBR) and the TLB is updated; physical address = <f, d>.
