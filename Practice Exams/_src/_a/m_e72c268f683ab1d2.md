(a) Correlate sensor reports into a vehicle-passage ID; atomically check-and-insert a unique charge record in a database transaction. A mutex/serializable transaction protects deduplication; a shared unique key also prevents duplicate charges across servers and retries.\
(b) Trigger reader activation on vehicle arrival, adapt RF power/duty cycle, use RFID anticollision inventory, and sleep between vehicles; the passive eTag itself has no battery to conserve.\
(c) Automatic recognition at a gate/edge processor for low latency and reduced video traffic; uncertain cases go to a central server and human review station with stored images and correlated eTag evidence.
