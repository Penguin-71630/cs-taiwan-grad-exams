Keep two doubly linked lists (deques): front half $F$ and back half $B$ with $|F| = \lceil n/2 \rceil$; the middle is the tail of $F$. ENQUEUE appends to $B$, DEQUEUE removes the head of $F$, EXTRACT_MID removes the tail of $F$; then move at most one element between the tail of $F$ and the head of $B$ to rebalance. All $O(1)$
