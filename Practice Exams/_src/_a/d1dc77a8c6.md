All target problems are in NP; use these polynomial reductions:\
(a) Add a new vertex $z$ adjacent only to the prescribed endpoints $a,b$; deleting $z$ from a Hamiltonian cycle gives exactly an $a$–$b$ Hamiltonian path, and conversely.\
(b) On the same $n$ vertices, form a complete graph with weight $1$ for original edges and $2$ for nonedges; a tour of cost at most $n$ exists exactly when the original graph has a Hamiltonian cycle.\
(c) Keep the graph and set $D=2$; a spanning tree of maximum degree at most $2$ is exactly a Hamiltonian path.
